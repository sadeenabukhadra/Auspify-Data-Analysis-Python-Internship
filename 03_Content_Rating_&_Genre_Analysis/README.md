# Netflix Content Rating & Genre Analysis

## Objective
Analyze Netflix content ratings and genres to understand audience targeting and identify patterns across different content types.

## Steps Performed

### 1. Analyze Rating Categories
Analyzed the distribution of content ratings to identify the most common rating categories in the Netflix dataset.

### 2. Identify the Most Common Genres
Analyzed the genre information to determine the most frequently represented content genres on Netflix.

### 3. Compare Ratings Across Content Types
Compared rating distributions between Movies and TV Shows to identify differences in content targeting.

### 4. Data Visualization
Created interactive visualizations (Plotly) to represent rating distributions, genre popularity, and rating differences between Movies and TV Shows.

### 5. Audience Insights
Analyzed the results to understand Netflix's content targeting patterns and identify important audience segments based on ratings, genres, and content types.

## Key Findings
- **TV-MA** is the most common rating (36.46% of titles), followed by **TV-14** (24.54%) — together over 60% of the catalog skews toward mature/teen audiences.
- **International Movies**, **Dramas**, and **Comedies** are the top genres, reflecting a global, drama/comedy-led content strategy.
- **TV Shows skew more mature than Movies**: TV-MA makes up 42.91% of TV Shows vs. 33.66% of Movies.
- Family-rated content (G, TV-G, TV-Y) is a comparatively small share of the library.

## Tools Used
Python, Pandas, Plotly

## Dataset
`Dataset.csv` — Netflix titles dataset (rating, listed_in/genre, and type columns)
